Application Document Checklist

Academic Documents

  1. Student ID/Registration Card
  2. Household Registration (Family List)
  3. Matriculation Certificate
  4. Matriculation Transcript/Mark Sheet
  5. Matriculation Pass Certificate (confirmation letter)
  6. University Yearly Transcripts
  7. University Degree Certificate

All academic documents above require Korean Embassy seal/certification.

Identification Documents

  1. Parents’ NRC (Father & Mother)

Financial Documents

  1. Parents’ Annual Income Certificate
  2. Parents’ Business License / Property Ownership Documents
  3. Bank Statement (Show Money) — minimum 6 months account history

D4 Visa: Myanmar Kyat equivalent to USD $10,000
D2 Visa: Myanmar Kyat equivalent to USD $20,000

Supplementary Documents

  1. Course Certificates / Employment Verification Letter (for education gap period, if applicable)
  2. Korean Language Certificate (e.g., TOPIK)
